🧮 Recalculate / Reconcile Data (Admin)
Monthly consumption is always derived live from the Issue History, so any reversed transaction is already removed from that total automatically. This tool is a safety net: it rebuilds every material's stock quantity from its opening balance plus all receipts minus all issues. Use it to fix any drift caused by manual edits, partial restores or stale cloud data.

Formula applied: quantity = openingBalance + Σ receipts − Σ issues
🔄 Reversal & Recalculation Log — Audit Trail
Every reversed receipt or issue is archived here together with the exact stock adjustment that was applied. Recalculations are also recorded. Reversed transactions are removed from the live history, from the monthly-consumption totals and from the dashboard KPI cards, so stock, history and reports always stay consistent.
